Personal automation project

Tiktok videos

A personal, single-user automation that publishes my own pre-recorded DJ Scirocco music mix videos to my own TikTok account on a schedule.

How it works

  • Video mixes are produced ahead of time and stored in a local library.
  • An automated workflow (built with n8n) selects an eligible video by name and length.
  • The workflow authenticates with my own TikTok account via OAuth2 and publishes the video through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Who this is for

  • This is not a public service. It has exactly one operator and one connected TikTok account.
