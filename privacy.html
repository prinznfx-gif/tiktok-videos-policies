Tiktok videos — personal automation tool

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 July 2026
In short: This tool has exactly one user — its own operator. It does not collect data from, or about, anyone else. It stores only what is required to publish the operator's own videos to the operator's own TikTok account, and it never sells or shares data with third parties.

01Overview

"Tiktok videos" is a single-user backend automation, built with n8n, that publishes the operator's own pre-recorded videos to the operator's own TikTok account on a schedule. It is not a public website or app, has no visitors or sign-ups, and processes no personal data belonging to anyone other than its operator.

02What data is processed

DataWhy
TikTok OAuth access & refresh tokensAuthenticate the operator's own account to TikTok's Content Posting API
Local video filesRead from the operator's own folder in order to be uploaded and published
Filenames & durationsUsed to decide which video is eligible for posting

No analytics, advertising identifiers, or data about other TikTok users is collected at any point.

03Storage & security

OAuth tokens are stored inside the operator's own local n8n instance and are never transmitted anywhere other than directly to TikTok's official API endpoints. Video files remain on the operator's own machine before and after publishing.

04Third-party services

The only external service this tool communicates with is TikTok's own Content Posting API. No other third party, analytics provider, or advertising network receives any data from this tool.

05Retention & revocation

Tokens are retained for as long as the tool remains in use. The operator can revoke access at any time from TikTok's account settings, which immediately invalidates the tool's ability to post.

06Children's privacy

This tool is a personal, single-operator utility and is not directed at, marketed to, or used by children.

07Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as the tool evolves. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
